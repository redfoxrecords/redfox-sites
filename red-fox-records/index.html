About

Built by listening

Red Fox Records is an independent music curation project born between Berlin and Brazil. We build genre-focused Spotify playlists, rediscover overlooked tracks and give emerging artists a place alongside established names.

Every selection is editorial. No paid placement. No guaranteed spots. No bots, follow-gates or artificial growth. Just music we believe belongs there.

We like songs with identity — whether they come from a bedroom, a rehearsal room or an established catalogue.

Get involved

Submit your music

Independent artists are welcome. We listen to every submission and select tracks according to each playlist's editorial direction.

Submission is free. Placement is never sold or guaranteed.

Send us a streaming link and a short introduction via Instagram or email.

Submission guidelines
We prioritize music performed and produced by real artists. AI can be part of the creative process, but fully AI-generated final productions are outside our editorial line.
